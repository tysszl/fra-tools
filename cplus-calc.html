Configuration

The Component Plus Line

Component Plus is the three-product commodity-pairing line: calcium nitrate (CaNO3), Component Plus (C+), and monopotassium phosphate (MKP). C+ (2-16-20 with 8% Mg, 11% S, and a full chelated micronutrient package) is the backbone; CaNO3 and MKP supply the calcium and phosphorus it deliberately omits. The C+ line has no Stretch recipe — Stretch-week EC targets run the Stack recipe. C+ stock concentrate must be used within 14 days of mixing.

Application

Stock Concentrate is the standard commercial setup. Fertilizer is pre-mixed into concentrated stock tanks, then injected into the irrigation line by dosing equipment (injectors). The calculator shows how much product to add to each stock tank and the injection rates for your dosers.

Direct to Reservoir is for facilities that mix nutrients directly into a reservoir or batch tank at working strength — no stock tanks or injectors involved. The calculator shows how much product to add per gallon (or per liter) of final solution.

Dosers

3-Doser: Three separate stock tanks — CaNO3, Component Plus, and MKP each get their own injector, mixed at 1 lb per gallon (1-1-1). This is the standard setup and allows full flexibility with per-phase recipe adjustments. Injection rates differ between tanks since each product carries a different share of the total EC.

2-Doser: Two stock tanks — CaNO3 alone in Tank 1, and C+ + MKP combined in Tank 2 at 0.75 + 1.00 lb/gal. Used in facilities with only two injectors. Calcium can never share a tank with phosphate or sulfate, so CaNO3 always stays alone. Choose 0.75 lb/gal CaNO3 for equal-rate operation or 1.00 lb/gal for a whole-bag build with separate injector rates. The combined tank fixes the C+-to-MKP ratio, so flower runs Swell with an optional Near Ripen final phase. Confirm with EC/PPM validation.

Final Phase

Near Ripen holds calcium at the canonical Ripen share (31.5% of total EC), then assigns the remaining 68.5% to the fixed C+ + MKP tank. It is a two-tank approximation, not the canonical three-product Ripen recipe.

Component Plus stock mixes at 1 lb per gallon in each of the three tanks (1-1-1). Injection rates differ between tanks — each product carries a different share of the total EC.

Recipe Schedule

Commercial (Stack → Swell) — the default chart — is the simplified production program: Veg / Moms on Veg, the Stack recipe through early flower (Wk 1–2), then Swell for the remainder, finishing on Ripen. The Swell phase steps down through mid-flower: weeks 3–5 run at the higher Swell EC (2.7 High / 2.2 Standard), weeks 6–8/9 at the lower Swell EC (2.4 High / 2.0 Standard), before Ripen. Custom EC values override this.

Swell Through Flower keeps Veg / Moms on the Veg recipe, then runs the Swell recipe for the entire flower cycle — the simplest single-recipe flower program. As modern growing methods (high-intensity LED, under-canopy lighting, precision irrigation) have raised average feed EC, Swell delivers more favorable outcomes at those higher EC levels. Target EC still follows the selected EC preset or your custom EC values.

Custom appears when individual recipe dropdowns in the feed schedule have been changed manually.

2-Doser mode runs the fixed C+ + MKP tank on Swell through flower. The separate Final Phase control can hold Swell or taper calcium to Near Ripen.

Supplements

PhosZyme is added to its carrier stock tank at 10% of the Component Plus weight — 5 lb per 50-lb C+ tank — which keeps the applied dose at or near the 0.4 g/gal target across the chart. The carrier is the Component Plus tank (in 2-doser mode, the combined C+ + MKP tank). PhosZyme is not calcium-compatible, so it never goes in a tank with CaNO3. It contains phosphorus-digesting enzymes that improve nutrient availability. When enabled, the calculator automatically scales all dosages down slightly so the total EC — including PhosZyme's contribution — still hits your target. Toggle it on if your feed program includes PhosZyme.

Si is a silica supplement used as a foliar spray at 0.5–2 mL/gal, once weekly, from veg through week 3 of flower.

pH Up is used as needed to raise pH into the target range measured at the dripper. On the Component Plus line the flower recipes (Stack, Swell) run 5.5–5.7; Veg and Ripen run 5.5–6.0. Lines running above 25 °C (77 °F) want the low end of the range. Maximum 0.2–0.25 g/gal total in the mix. Always add last, after all nutrients are mixed, in 0.05 g/gal increments, and allow 5–15 minutes of reaction time before re-measuring — approach the target gradually and stop when you reach it. The pH Up calculator's recipe rows are built on the 3-Part line; do not use them as a Component Plus dose.

BioFlo is a bioenzymatic line cleaner that removes biofilm from irrigation lines, maintaining consistent flow and even nutrient delivery. Not a feed additive — used as a periodic soak between or during cycles.

Triologic is a microbial inoculant containing Mycorrhization Helper Bacteria. It amplifies mycorrhizal colonization when applied as a weekly hand-water drench alongside a separate mycorrhizal inoculant.
