Verify at the dripper

The calculation is the applied dose. Measure free chlorine at the farthest dripper and maintain the current FRA target of 0.5–2 ppm.

ORP is useful for tracking sanitation over time (300–500 mV at the dripper), but it can't stand in for a free chlorine test. Use a free chlorine reading to set and check the dose.

Avoid

  • Targeting 2–3 ppm at the dripper. The continuous-use range is 0.5–2 ppm free chlorine.
  • Setting or adjusting the dose from ORP alone.
  • Mixing calcium hypochlorite with peroxides or other strong oxidizers in a reservoir.

Fertilizer stock concentrate guidance

Keep the applied chlorine dose at or below 2 ppm based on the final fertilizer-stock volume. Add it only after roughly 90% of the final water volume is already in the tank, then complete filling and mixing. This avoids a brief localized overconcentration.

The 2 ppm limit applies to the finished fertilizer stock concentrate. It does not limit the concentration of a separate DryTec dosing stock.

DryTec handling and mixing
  • Use clean, dry tools.
  • Add DryTec to water. Never add water to DryTec.
  • Wear chemical-resistant gloves and eye protection.
  • Avoid breathing dust and use adequate ventilation.
  • Store DryTec cool and dry in its original container.
  • Keep DryTec away from acids, organic material, and other incompatible products.

Calculated chlorine values are theoretical applied doses. Field decisions use measured free chlorine at the applicable endpoint.
